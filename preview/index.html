01 / A LITTLE ABOUT MEGUANGZHOU, CHINA

Building the Reasoning Foundation of Physical AI

Let’s exchange ideas↗

I’m Kun Xiang (项鲲), a PhD candidate at Sun Yat-sen University. I work on AI that can reason about the physical world and learn by interacting with it.

I am advised by Prof. Xiaodan Liang and am currently a research intern at Alibaba DAMO Academy.

My research connects multimodal reasoning, physical world modeling, and agentic reinforcement learning. I explore how models interpret visual evidence, adapt the depth of their reasoning, and refine their decisions through feedback.

RESEARCH DIRECTIONS
