Thanks for stopping by! 👋


I am currently a PhD student at the [HCP-I2 Lab](https://www.sysu-hcp.net) in Sun Yat-sen University advised by [Prof. Xiaodan Liang](https://lemondan.github.io/). Previously, I obtained both my Bachelor's and Master's degree at SYSU under the supervision of [Prof. Shancheng Jiang](https://ise.sysu.edu.cn/teacher/teacher02/1415334.htm).

Currently, I am interested in building generalizable multimodal reasoning systems grounded in data-centric perspective. I am working to answer: 

1) **Inward**: How can MLLMs enhance perception and reasoning for real-world comprehension?
2) **Outward**: How can MLLMs enable modeling and interaction for physical engagement?
